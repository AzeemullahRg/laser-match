Laser Match Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Developer: Meeza Games · Contact: azeemzafar67@gmail.com

Laser Match is a puzzle game. You do not create an account, and the game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, best moves), your settings (music, sound, gentle motion), and your daily gift, streak and star-hint balance are saved in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive this information. Android may include it in your own device backup if you have backup turned on. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a hint, and occasional full-screen ads between levels (never before level 3 is completed, and never in the middle of a level). If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

To show, measure and protect ads, Google's ad software collects: your device's IP address (which can indicate your general location), interactions with ads and the app, app and SDK performance data, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. All of it is sent encrypted. We treat every player as possibly under the age of consent and ask Google to apply its restricted ad treatment accordingly. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy Remove Ads once. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The game checks with Google Play on your device whether you own Remove Ads.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard and whether players come back, the game sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: app opens, days since you first played, levels started, finished or left (with the number of moves and time taken), hints used, rewards claimed, and ads shown. Google Analytics also records a random app-instance ID, an approximate location derived from a masked IP address, and Google Play purchase events (product and price).

Analytics is switched off until Google's consent check allows it, and we do not use it for advertising: collection of the advertising ID by Analytics is turned off and the advertising consent signals are set to "denied". Analytics data is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play), which provides these services, under Google's terms and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

Laser Match is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and deletion requests

  • Change ad consent at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices (where consent applies).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the game removes everything stored on your device.
  • To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, email azeemzafar67@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
